# Design References

This document records visual inputs for Kyle Cromwell's personal website. It does not approve a visual direction or authorize site changes. `PRODUCT.md` is the approved product context; `DESIGN.md` and the current tokens remain unapproved.

The reference files live in `src/assets/design/inspiration/`.

## Shared Read

The three references use a political field-atlas language: editorial display type, technical metadata, asymmetric composition, disciplined negative space, and a restrained research-document mood.

That language is useful as a set of compositional tools, not as a template. Taken literally, it would turn a friendly college-student introduction into a fictional intelligence dossier. The eventual direction should preserve Kyle's curiosity and political-science interests without making him feel severe, institutional, or performative.

## Reference 1: Julian Thorne Homepage Typography

- **File:** [`julian-thorne-homepage-typography.png`](../src/assets/design/inspiration/julian-thorne-homepage-typography.png)
- **Source URL:** Not available. Supplied directly by Vivian in `design/inspiration/`.
- **Composition worth carrying forward:** One dominant typographic gesture surrounded by substantial negative space. Small edge annotations and an offset supporting statement create secondary paths through the page without competing with the main idea.
- **Typography worth carrying forward:** Extreme scale contrast between a high-contrast display face, compact utility text, and a quiet italic note. The hierarchy feels intentional before any copy is read.
- **Color temperature worth carrying forward:** Cool aubergine and dusty lavender create an introspective atmosphere. A small, warmer red accent prevents the palette from feeling inert.
- **Texture worth carrying forward:** The faint diagonal grid and soft tonal variation make the background feel tactile without becoming illustration.
- **Density worth carrying forward:** Sparse at headline scale, dense only in tiny metadata. This creates rhythm instead of uniform minimalism.
- **Mood worth carrying forward:** Curious, cerebral, cinematic, and slightly mysterious.
- **Do not copy literally:** The exact fractured name treatment, the all-purple canvas, invented subject-status labels, fake coordinates, edge codes, or the precise diagonal-grid pattern. Do not reproduce the composition as a nameplate with swapped-in Kyle copy.

## Reference 2: Architecture of Power Editorial Layout

- **File:** [`architecture-of-power-editorial-layout.png`](../src/assets/design/inspiration/architecture-of-power-editorial-layout.png)
- **Source URL:** Not available. Supplied directly by Vivian in `design/inspiration/`.
- **Composition worth carrying forward:** A strongly asymmetric split between a large visual field and a compact observation panel. Generous outer margins, disciplined alignment, and thin rules establish order without relying on cards.
- **Typography worth carrying forward:** A clear editorial hierarchy with a prominent serif title, restrained body copy, and small metadata. The Roman numeral works as a navigational landmark rather than decoration.
- **Color temperature worth carrying forward:** Warm off-white, soft charcoal, and a muted rust accent feel archival and humane rather than stark.
- **Texture worth carrying forward:** Subtle paper warmth and hairline rules suggest a physical research folio.
- **Density worth carrying forward:** Low overall density with information concentrated into deliberate clusters. Empty space carries as much weight as the content.
- **Mood worth carrying forward:** Reflective, academic, calm, and observant.
- **Do not copy literally:** The exact two-column proportions, vertical border text, fake publication data, pill tags used only as decoration, Roman numerals on every section, or an empty image frame. The final site must use real travel imagery where the content calls for it.

## Reference 3: Tactical Play Editorial Layout

- **File:** [`tactical-play-editorial-layout.png`](../src/assets/design/inspiration/tactical-play-editorial-layout.png)
- **Source URL:** Not available. Supplied directly by Vivian in `design/inspiration/`.
- **Composition worth carrying forward:** A dramatic 40/60 relationship between a large title block and a decisive image. The title and image counterbalance each other rather than sitting in a centered stack.
- **Typography worth carrying forward:** Oversized display type establishes the subject immediately, while a compact paragraph provides context without diluting the composition.
- **Color temperature worth carrying forward:** Cool plum, muted lavender, and monochrome imagery create focus. The tiny red registration mark adds tension without becoming a second palette.
- **Texture worth carrying forward:** A dark matte field, faint grid, and framed photographic area make the page feel layered.
- **Density worth carrying forward:** One image and one statement dominate; technical details remain peripheral.
- **Mood worth carrying forward:** Immersive, analytical, personal, and quietly intense.
- **Do not copy literally:** The exact gaming-station crop, the full dark-purple treatment, fake telemetry and coordinates, the precise frame treatment, or the same grid used across every page. Kyle's interest in games should not become the site's entire identity.

## Cross-Reference Boundaries

- Do not copy the editorial field-atlas lane wholesale. It is already a recognizable aesthetic formula.
- Do not let political-science styling make Kyle appear institutional, militaristic, or humorless.
- Do not use fabricated metadata to simulate substance.
- Do not make purple automatically mean a dark or drenched theme.
- Do not sacrifice clear access to Home, Visited Countries, Interesting Places, and About Me for novelty.
- Do not replace Kyle's existing travel imagery with decorative placeholders or generated substitutes.

## Approved Direction Inputs

- **Visual lane:** Approachable personal field journal.
- **Theme rhythm:** Alternating light and dark sections rather than a single theme across the whole site.
- **Atmosphere:** Curious and collegiate.
- **Color strategy:** Purple is committed within selected dark sections and restrained on light sections.
- **Background treatment:** Purple sections may use a subtle geometric field pattern inspired by the references. The pattern should add depth and structure without reducing readability.

These choices should keep the site personal and welcoming while allowing moments of archival structure and immersion. The light and dark sections need a shared typographic and spacing system so the alternation feels like one journal rather than two unrelated sites. Any geometric background treatment must be an original interpretation, not a reproduction of the references' exact diagonal grid.

These decisions are approved inputs, not an implemented design. Site code, `DESIGN.md`, and design tokens must remain unchanged until Vivian approves a later design proposal.
