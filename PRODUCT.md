# Product Context

## Product Purpose

This is a personal website for Kyle Cromwell, a college student. It helps visitors get to know him through his academic interests, personal interests, travels, and older creative pursuits.

The site should make Kyle feel thoughtful, curious, and interesting as a whole person. It is not a resume, professional portfolio, travel guide, or ranked list of destinations.

## Register

Brand. The visitor's understanding of Kyle is the product.

## Audience

The site is for classmates, instructors, future contacts, friends, family, and curious visitors. No single professional audience takes priority.

## Kyle

Kyle studies political science. He loves great food, plays video games, and enjoys spending time with friends.

## Voice

Thoughtful and curious.

The writing should feel personal and clear without becoming overly casual, promotional, or academically formal.

## Site Map

The primary pages are:

- Home
- Visited Countries
- Interesting Places
- About Me

There is no Work section or case study in the current site map.

Chocolate and Poems are secondary archive pages. They represent older interests and are linked from About Me rather than treated as primary navigation destinations.

## Page Roles

### Home

Home introduces Kyle through both academic and personal interests.

Visited Countries and Interesting Places must be directly and prominently accessible from Home. About Me must also be accessible from Home, but in a quieter secondary role.

### Visited Countries

Use the website's existing country content. The page is a personal record of places Kyle has visited. No new country content is currently planned.

### Interesting Places

Use the website's existing place descriptions and media. No new place content is currently planned.

### About Me

About Me introduces Kyle as a political science student who enjoys great food, video games, and time with friends.

It also provides quieter links to the Chocolate and Poems archives.

The contact address is displayed in an obfuscated form to reduce automated scraping:

`kylecromwell2023 [at] gmail [dot] com`

There is no contact form or primary contact call to action at this stage.

## Navigation Requirements

Navigation does not need to follow a traditional header-menu pattern. A more interesting interaction may be explored later.

Regardless of its eventual form, navigation must remain understandable and must make the four primary pages easy to find. The navigation interaction and visual form are not decided in this document.

## Content Constraints

- Reuse the existing Visited Countries and Interesting Places content.
- Do not invent new travel entries, memories, academic work, or biographical details.
- Do not add a Work section, case study, resume framing, or primary call to action.
- Keep Chocolate and Poems available as secondary archives.

## Success Criteria

- A visitor quickly understands that Kyle is a college student with both academic and personal interests.
- Kyle comes across as thoughtful, curious, and interesting.
- Visited Countries and Interesting Places are immediately discoverable from Home.
- About Me is discoverable from Home without competing with the two primary content destinations.
- Chocolate and Poems remain accessible without appearing as primary sections.
- Visitors can find Kyle's obfuscated email address without exposing a plain scrape-friendly address.

## Design Status

Visual direction is intentionally undecided.

`DESIGN.md`, the starter tokens, and files under `src/assets/design/inspiration/` are not approved design context. The inspiration files are visual inputs for a later design phase and must not be analyzed or used to choose a direction during product initialization.
